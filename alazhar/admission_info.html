Documents

What You Need to Apply

Please bring the following documents along with the completed admission form.

2 copies of passport-size colour photographs of the student and a photocopy of the birth registration certificate.

Photocopy of the National ID (NID) card of the father or mother.

Transfer certificate (release letter) from the previous institution for students of Class 3 to Class 9.

Guidelines

Madrasah Rules

To ensure smooth academic discipline, all guardians are requested to follow the tuition fee schedule below.

  1. The tuition fee for the current month must be paid between the 1st and 7th of every English month.

  2. In case of special difficulty, the tuition fee may be paid without any penalty up to the 10th.

  3. From the 11th onwards, an additional 10 (ten) Taka per day will be charged as a late fine along with the regular tuition fee.

Timely payment helps us maintain the quality of education, meals and residential care for every student.
