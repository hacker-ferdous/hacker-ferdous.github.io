About Us

A Sanctuary of Quranic Learning & Modern Education

Al Azhar Madrasah unites traditional Islamic education — Hifz, Nazira, Fiqh and Akhlaq — with modern academic subjects, so our students flourish in both Deen and Dunya.

  • All-in-One: complete learning inside the classroom — no private tutor, coaching or extra lessons needed
  • Exclusive Nursing Class for new and comparatively weaker students in English, Arabic & Mathematics
  • Specially managed Hifz cum Academic programme — 80% Hifz and 20% core academics, with comfortable hostel & healthy meals
  • Insha'Allah, complete Hifz alongside core academics by 4th / 5th / 6th grade
  • Full fluency in English & Arabic by 8th grade
  • Ability to analyse Al-Quran & Al-Hadith by 10th grade
  • Eligibility for renowned universities, engineering universities & medical colleges at home and abroad after higher secondary
  • Debating & Language Club (Arabic, English & Bengali)
  • Rich library of subject-wise reference & core books; Science & Computer labs
  • 24-hour CCTV security & class monitoring; projectors and smart monitors in every classroom
  • Own transport service for students
  • All forms of corporal punishment strictly prohibited

Our Programs

Paths of Learning

From the first letters of the Quran to complete Hifz and higher Islamic studies — a path for every age.

Maktab

An introduction to basic Quran reading, daily duas and Islamic manners for young children.

Nurani

Easy and accurate Quran recitation through the Nurani method in a short span of time.

Nazira

Reading practice from the Quran with correct makhraj, tajwid and pronunciation.

Hifz

A complete Quran memorization programme with daily sabaq, sabqi and manzil.

Shunani

A special method of strengthening memorization through listening and repetition.

Husne Sawt

Training in melodious and beautiful Quranic recitation.

Worldly Objectives

  • By higher secondary (Grade 12), students of both general and Islamic streams will be enriched with globalization-ready practical and fundamental knowledge in every subject.
  • To prepare students to qualify for the University of Dhaka, medical colleges, BUET and seats of higher learning at home and abroad.
  • To build ideal, moral, patriotic and emerging entrepreneurs and enterprising individuals.
  • To serve the country and the nation by producing both general scholars and scholars of the Quran and Hadith.

Ultimate Goal

Success and well-being in this world and the Hereafter! Salvation from the punishment of Jahannam!
