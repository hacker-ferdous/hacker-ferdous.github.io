Smart Electric Vehicle Charging Station Using Solar Energy

Author: Shomrat Mohammad Yousuf Ali Shibli | Institute: Bangladesh Army University of Science & Technology (BAUST), Saidpur

Abstract

The growing demand for electricity and electric vehicles (EVs) has increased the need for sustainable and intelligent charging infrastructure. This project proposes a solar-powered wireless EV charging system utilizing parking spaces in offices, university campuses, and shopping malls. The system integrates wireless power transfer with solar photovoltaic technology for convenient and eco-friendly charging. It features automated entry and exit gates, RFID-based access control, and an LCD for displaying system information. A battery monitoring system tracks battery conditions, while an emergency power cutoff system enhances operational safety. Siren lights and sound provide emergency alerts, and smart road lighting improves visibility and energy efficiency. The proposed system aims to combine renewable energy, automation, safety, and smart parking management to promote sustainable transportation.

Objectives
  • To develop a solar-powered wireless EV charging system by integrating solar photovoltaic technology with wireless power transfer.
  • To utilize available parking spaces in offices, university campuses, and shopping malls as convenient wireless EV charging stations.
  • To improve charging convenience and efficiency by enabling cable-free charging of electric vehicles while parked.
  • To promote sustainable transportation and renewable energy utilization by reducing dependence on fossil fuels and minimizing carbon emissions.
  • To develop an automated parking system with RFID access, automatic gates, LCD monitoring, battery monitoring, emergency cutoff, siren alerts, and smart lighting.

Result & Discussion

The proposed system integrates solar photovoltaic energy with wireless power transfer for cable-free EV charging. Parking spaces function as smart charging zones, while RFID-based access control and automated gates support organized vehicle entry. LCD monitoring provides real-time system information, while battery monitoring and an emergency power cut-off system improve operational safety. Signal lights and audible alerts provide warnings, and smart lighting enhances visibility. Overall, integrating renewable energy, automation, and safety features demonstrates a feasible smart charging infrastructure model, reducing dependence on conventional grid electricity.

Conclusion

The proposed smart electric vehicle charging station using solar energy provides an integrated approach to sustainable, intelligent EV charging. By combining renewable energy with wireless power transfer, the system enables convenient, cable-free charging. RFID access, automated gates, monitoring, emergency protection, and smart lighting improve automation and safety. By utilizing existing parking spaces, this approach reduces fossil fuel dependence and supports cleaner transportation. Overall, it offers a safe, efficient, and environmentally sustainable charging infrastructure concept.
